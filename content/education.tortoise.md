# Education

I can still remember my first day of school. People say people don't usually remember the very early days of their life. I don't know how I do.

I remember I was sitting beside the window, looking at the clouds and how they moved, and my teacher, how she was holding the chalkboard and smiled! Just beside the window, how the shrubs and vines were swaying and the morning sunlight was kissing them...

Since then I was getting to know the world with the predefined words the world already has. I changed schools 5 times. I was thinking I might be an engineer. But when I completed college, the time was knocking to decide what to become, to choose among subjects to get admitted to a university.

I thought, well, engineering is not for me. Then for a year I found myself reading and writing poetry and literature. So I got myself admitted to coaching, so that I could get a chance at a top university with a beautiful subject like Literature, Psychology or Geography, which were of my interest. I was doing very well at the coaching.

One fine morning, somehow I had a feeling: am I doing right? All my regular books, printed lecture sheets and notebooks were filled with sketches.

Just a memory of my college: I once stood for a whole period in my class as I was drawing something. I introduced a pagebook. A comic book where our classmates commented or posted a status, just like a status on any social media. But things were physical. A physical notebook, colorful pens, interesting statuses drawn by anyone in the class. Ha ha... Also my whole school life was colorful, with tons of interesting activities.

Btw, I came back home from the coaching. I chose to get admitted to fine arts. I did my honors and masters, majoring in drawing and painting.

My masters submission was interactive. I tried to create something so that it wouldn't become just a painting. It would become an experience for the viewers.

I started making book covers for writers around me.
